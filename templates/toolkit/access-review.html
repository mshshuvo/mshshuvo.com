Review settings

1. Staff list

From HR: employee_id, name, email, status, termination_date. Department and job_title help reviewers.

2. Access exports

One file per system, or one for all: system, account, email or employee_id, roles, status, last_login. Service accounts: account_type and owner.

3. Role map

What each role lets someone do: system, role, function, privileged. A role can have several rows.

4. Segregation of duties rules

Pairs of functions one person shouldn't hold: rule_id, function_a, function_b, risk. Twelve starter rules are included.
